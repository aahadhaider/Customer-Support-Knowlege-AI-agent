# FIN Customer Support FAQ Knowledge Base

## CATEGORY: Account & Registration

**Q: How do I register as a micro-entrepreneur on the platform?**
A: Download the FIN app, click "Register as Partner", enter your Aadhaar number, mobile number, and complete KYC verification. Approval takes 24–48 hours.

**Q: My account is blocked. What should I do?**
A: Account blocks happen due to suspicious transactions or incomplete KYC. Contact support with your registered mobile number and we will review within 2 business hours.

**Q: How do I reset my MPIN?**
A: Open the app → Settings → Security → Reset MPIN. You will receive an OTP on your registered number. If you don't receive OTP, check network and try again after 5 minutes.

**Q: Can I have two accounts with the same mobile number?**
A: No. One mobile number is linked to one account only. If you need a new account, contact support.

## CATEGORY: Transactions & Payments

**Q: My money transfer failed but amount was deducted from my account. What do I do?**
A: Failed transactions are automatically reversed within 2–4 hours. If not reversed after 4 hours, raise a complaint with the transaction ID. We will resolve within 24 hours.

**Q: What is the daily transaction limit?**
A: The default daily limit is ₹50,000 for new partners. After 3 months of active usage, the limit increases to ₹2,00,000. Premium partners can request higher limits.

**Q: How do I check my commission earnings?**
A: Go to App → My Wallet → Commission History. You can filter by date range and service type.

**Q: When are commissions credited to my wallet?**
A: Commissions are credited in real-time for most services. Bill payment commissions are credited within 24 hours.

**Q: A customer paid but I didn't receive confirmation. What happened?**
A: Check your transaction history first. If the transaction shows "Pending", wait 10 minutes. If still pending after 30 minutes, escalate with the transaction reference number.

## CATEGORY: Services

**Q: Which bill payment services are available?**
A: Electricity, water, gas, broadband, DTH, mobile recharge, insurance premiums, and municipal taxes across 500+ billers.

**Q: How do I add a new biller that isn't listed?**
A: Go to Bill Payments → Request New Biller → Fill the form with biller details. We add approved billers within 7–10 business days.

**Q: Is Aadhaar-based cash withdrawal available in my area?**
A: AePS (Aadhaar-enabled Payment System) is available in most pin codes. Check availability: App → Services → AePS → Check Availability → Enter pin code.

**Q: How do I apply for a micro-loan for my customer?**
A: Go to Financial Services → Micro Loans → Enter customer Aadhaar and mobile → Customer completes e-KYC → Loan disbursed in 10 minutes if eligible.

## CATEGORY: Technical Issues

**Q: The app is crashing or not loading.**
A: 1) Clear app cache (Settings → Apps → Eko → Clear Cache). 2) Check internet connection. 3) Uninstall and reinstall if issue persists. 4) Minimum Android 6.0 required.

**Q: Biometric login is not working.**
A: Biometric login requires AePS device registration. Go to Settings → Biometric → Re-register Device. Ensure your fingerprint scanner is clean and dry.

**Q: OTP is not coming.**
A: 1) Check if DND is enabled on your SIM. 2) Check network signal. 3) Try "Resend OTP" after 60 seconds. 4) If using dual SIM, check which SIM is registered with us.

## CATEGORY: Commissions & Earnings

**Q: My commission rate was reduced. Why?**
A: Commission rates are revised quarterly based on volume targets. You can view current rates in App → Profile → Commission Structure. Maintaining higher monthly volumes unlocks better rates.

**Q: How do I withdraw my wallet balance?**
A: App → My Wallet → Withdraw → Enter bank account details → Amount is credited within 2–4 hours on working days.

**Q: I referred a friend but didn't get referral bonus.**
A: Referral bonus is credited after your referral completes 10 transactions. Check status: App → Refer & Earn → My Referrals.
